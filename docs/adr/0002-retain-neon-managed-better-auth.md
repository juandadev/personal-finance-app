# Retain Neon Managed Better Auth for the closed beta

The Mexico-only invitation beta will retain Neon Managed Better Auth despite its beta status and lack of MFA because the limited audience and controlled enrollment make that residual risk acceptable for this phase. Migration must be evaluated before broader availability or sooner if MFA becomes required, the service or support model materially changes, a security incident exposes an unacceptable dependency risk, or legal review imposes requirements the service cannot meet.
