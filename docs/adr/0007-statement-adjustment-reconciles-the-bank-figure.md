# Reconcile a credit-card statement with a Statement Adjustment

A credit-card statement in the app can disagree with the bank because of point redemptions or foreign-currency bills whose peso amount is not fixed. We record a Statement Adjustment that sets one unpaid statement to the bank's figure, instead of rewriting the original purchase or bill. A lower bank figure is a positive amount and a higher figure is a negative amount. Neither is income, an expense, or a budget movement, and the Primary Account moves only when the statement is later paid. Bringing the amount owed to zero, with nothing still pending, closes the statement without a cash payment.
