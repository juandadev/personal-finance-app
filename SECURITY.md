# Security Policy

## Supported Version

Only the currently deployed closed-beta version receives security fixes.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Report suspected vulnerabilities through the
private security contact listed in the application's privacy notice.

Include:

- the affected route or feature;
- reproducible steps with non-production data;
- the impact you observed; and
- a safe way to contact you.

Do not access another person's records, retain copied financial data, or
perform availability testing. We will acknowledge reports promptly, preserve
evidence, and follow the incident-response procedure in
`docs/security/incident-response.md`.
